## FreePats MuldjordKit (acoustic drum kit)

The drum sounds use nine unmodified hit samples from **MuldjordKit**, the acoustic drum kit recorded in 2010 by **Lars Muldjord** ([muldjord.com](https://muldjord.com)), in the FreePats edition (version 2020-10-18) packaged by `roberto@zenvoid.org`.

- Source: <https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Percussion/acoustic-drum-kit.html>
